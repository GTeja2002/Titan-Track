TitanTrack privacy

How TitanTrack uses your data, in plain words.

Step count (Health Connect)

On Android, if you allow it, TitanTrack reads only your daily step count from Health Connect. It does not read heart rate, location, workouts, sleep or any other health data, and it never writes anything to Health Connect.

Your step count is shown on your dashboard and saved with your daily log so you can see it on your other devices. You can turn this off at any time in the Health Connect app.

What TitanTrack stores

  • Your profile and goals (for example age, height, weight and goal, if you enter them).
  • Your daily logs: meals and foods, water, steps, activity, weight, and wellbeing check-ins.
  • Foods you add yourself with “+ What I Eat”.

This is saved on your device and, when you are signed in, in your private account database. Database rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your records.

What TitanTrack does not do

  • It does not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • It does not use your health data for advertising.
  • It does not show your data to other users.

Your control

  • Export: Profile → Your data lets you download everything (JSON or CSV).
  • Delete: Profile → Delete account permanently removes your account and all of its records.
  • Steps: remove TitanTrack’s access in the Health Connect app at any time.
